Convert Selenium IDE to Playwright

Drop a Selenium IDE .side file and get Playwright Test (TypeScript) code. HTML test suites saved by Katalon Recorder or Selenium IDE 1.x/2.x work too. The conversion runs in your browser — your file is never uploaded.

This is a preview of the first 10 steps. The free converter handles one test with up to 10 steps. To export long tests and whole projects at once, use the SideReplay extension (Pro). It also runs your .side tests in Chrome and fixes broken locators.
