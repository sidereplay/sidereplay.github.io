Thank you for getting Pro!

Your license key is on its way to the email address you used at checkout. It usually arrives within a minute.

Activate Pro

  1. Click the SideReplay icon in Chrome's toolbar to open the side panel.
  2. Click Get Pro at the top right.
  3. Paste your license key and click Activate.

You can use the same key in up to 3 browsers.

Can't find the key?

Check your spam folder, or open the customer portal and sign in with your checkout email to see your license key, invoices and subscription.
